## Intent

v2 of the Hermes desktop plugins film, built to travel on Twitter. Cold open: a wall of
anonymous, paraphrased complaints about AI apps and plugins piles up and glitches out (no real
usernames, no imitation of any social site's UI). Drop: "We heard you." Then six complaints, each
answered live in the real Hermes Desktop app: the complaint card flies into the composer and
becomes the prompt (match cut), and the footage shows the plugin being built. Synthwave reskin,
focus timer in the chat, Pulse remixed by talking to it, an editable flow, a Spend page, and a
Mission Control finale over busy background chats. Same world as v1: Nous navy, statue print, gold
dust, 118 BPM score.

## Message

Whatever bugs you about your AI app, ask Hermes and it rebuilds that part of itself, live.
