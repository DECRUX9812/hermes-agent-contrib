## Intent

A plugin demo of the real Hermes Desktop app that grabs attention on Twitter: Hermes writes
three desktop plugins live (a Pulse orb that flares while it thinks, an inline chart in the chat,
a Cyanotype theme that reskins the app). Story told through motion and real footage framed by
camera punch-ins; on-screen words are few, large and to the side (Apple principle: anything on
screen is meant to be read, excess pulled back). Same world as the Bot Mode brand film: Nous navy,
statue print, gold dust, 118 BPM score.

## Message

Ask Hermes for a feature and it builds it into the app as a plugin, live.
